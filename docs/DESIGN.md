# Design

The owner-provided HOMEWARD design specification is the reference. This milestone translates its visual direction while narrowing its feature scope to a preview.

Warm scrapbook surfaces, archival article clarity and restrained family-book styling share one visual language. Primary navigation: Home, My memories, Explore, Family books. Branded experience names are secondary labels; all six branches are welcomed in text without official symbols.

Tokens: paper #F6F2E9, surface #FFFCF6, ink #26342F, muted #5F665E, forest #365A4C, focus #245B70, control border #817B70. Georgia/Times headings contrast with Segoe UI/system body text. No remote fonts or images. The paper and book compositions are original typographic design studies, not historical objects or user content.

Homepage: introductory spread, paper-cover study, everyday-memory explanation, editorial collection preview, product principles. Interior screens explain planned functionality without fake controls or service records. The foundation notice stays visible, and homepage calls to action lead to real preview pages.

Desktop uses a 1200px content maximum and paired layouts. Below 700px, navigation wraps as a full-width row and content becomes single-column. This bounded foundation uses visible top navigation rather than introducing a second mobile navigation component.

Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA, not a claim of conformance. Semantic links, one h1, landmarks, skip navigation, focus handling, 44px primary controls and reduced-motion-aware transitions are implemented. Real screen-reader, real touch, 400% zoom and forced-color review remain separate manual checks.
