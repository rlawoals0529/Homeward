## Future editorial gate

Every article needs a named human reviewer, claim-level sources, publication/access dates, revision history and clear distinctions between documented history, recollection and folklore. Every asset needs creator, source URL, license/permission, attribution and permitted uses. Government hosting alone is not permission to reuse an image.

Do not invent testimony, credentials, service records, awards, endorsements or archival photography. Personal contributions require explicit permission; local memories do not automatically become editorial content. A code license does not license a veteran's memories.

Potential research starting point, not approval of claims: the Naval History and Heritage Command's [Crossing the Line collection](https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/nhhc/browse-by-topic/heritage/customs-and-traditions0/crossing-line.html).
